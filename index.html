Key assumptions

How the numbers above are worked out.

FBT exemption

Zero-emission vehicles under the LCT threshold ($89,332 in 2024–25) are exempt from FBT. PHEVs are excluded from FY2026.

GST saving

Your employer claims the GST on the purchase, saving you about 1/11th of the car price. Built into this calculator.

Residual value

Based on ATO statutory percentages. At lease end you can pay the residual, re-lease, or hand the car back.

Tax rates

Uses ATO individual income tax rates plus the 2% Medicare levy. Excludes HECS/HELP and other deductions or offsets.

Car loan comparison

"Standard car loan" assumes the same car at the same rate, repaid from after-tax income with no GST benefit.
